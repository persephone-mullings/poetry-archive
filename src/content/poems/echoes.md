---
title: Echoes
date: 2024-09-29
blurb: For Sam
published: true
---
We set down words like stones,
each one just so, chipped and dressed
Especially, and laid careful down 
to form the rounded well. 

Such slow pace is second nature 
to me, but still some inner urge, 
yet more natural still, craves pace, 
ease, excitement.

See now why I, with you, bandy words
Throwing them under-arm, think fast!
I love their very resonance, the sounds of us
Repeat to hear them echo up from the water.
