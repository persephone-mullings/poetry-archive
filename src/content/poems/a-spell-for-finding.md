title: A Spell for Finding
date: 2026-09-21
blurb: Stay up late....
published: true
---
Stay up late.
Wrap up warm; new coat, scarf, jumper.
Drive out into the rain looking for
the wolf moon. Marvel at the 
lights caught like paint in the 
rain. Clouds, trees buildings will
all block any moon view but
out in the flurry and exhilaration
you will find something. You 
won't know it right away, 
and you may not need it for 
a long while but keep a 
tight hold on it, please.

Hold on tight to it.
