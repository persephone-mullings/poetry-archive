Where is your lead?
What have you done with it?

Where are the copses where we would rest?

Where are the fields where we would run?

Where are the blue clouds in your eyes?

Or the grey in your fur?

Where is the catch in your growl?

Now you've wandered on, far away
And I whistle at the empty air.
