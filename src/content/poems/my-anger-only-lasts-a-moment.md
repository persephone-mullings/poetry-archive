title: My anger only lasts a moment.
date: 2026-09-21
blurb: My anger only lasts a moment....

My anger only lasts a moment.
I chase it like a dog
at a car and it
crashes into 
tearless sighs and
ground teeth.

Would I do better
to push harder?
Run it down and 
let it burn my lungs
when I catch it?

What would I say then?
Spluttering through 
ash and blood.
