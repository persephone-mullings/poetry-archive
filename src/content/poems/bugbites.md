Omenlike, the fleas came just 
a short while before you died. 
I had thought, with you gone 
they would vanish also.

Naive perhaps, but I couldn’t 
help but think it was unfair. 
Afterall, what else did I have 
of you? Why leave them?

And now the bites on my leg,
scarlet first, then a bruised, wine dark 
are fading into faint love-bite brown 
and disappearing
