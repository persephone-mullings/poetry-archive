title: Trappings
date: 2026-10-11
published: true
---
You see, I look back on
us now, and half-impose
myself, now, onto then.
It takes a moment, to
remind myself, and I 
take and take away, 
all the details, I
flatten 'him', me, till
he's paper thin and 
wonder: How did you not
see? What grip could 
you have on that?
Here, Now, I remind 
myself, of holding
eachother so tight
we felt our hearts
against the other's 
chests. I remember
talking with you as 
I never talked otherwise.

I learn to be tired of remembering it all again.
