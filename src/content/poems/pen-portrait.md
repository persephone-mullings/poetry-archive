Smudged with thumb prints, bleeding
through pages, the subtle 
frost effect of feathering. 
I write in pen for its’ mess.
Poems scrawled on the backs
of letters, drafted and discarded. 
A penned word floods and collapses 
into the page, sinks deep and appears 
a shadow when the page is turned. 
As I bring you onto paper
you’re obliterated into
the rush. I catch you, momentary,
in glimpses. Then the ink dries,
and you’re gone into
something new.
