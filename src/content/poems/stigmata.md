It’s hard to give up even now, 
I have to fight day and night at it.
The promise that one day, someone,

attentive, would take note of my 
bloody palms and take my wrists 
gently in hand, hold them up and 

examine my scars. They’d disapprove 
of course, but with that hint of pride
that I endured. How noble. Never made 
a fuss. 

But all that offers, the highest accolade
I’m given to want is,
a death with dignity. 

If I am to be denied any say in the matter,
If I am to be a man after all,
I won’t have it all be suffering. 

If I make it through this alive, don’t 
talk about my resilience or 
endurance, let it be hope, or passion or 
curiosity or even courage.

If we are to lionise scars, let me stare
and weep for pride at those taken 
on to make your body a home.
