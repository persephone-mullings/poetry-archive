title: Evenings
date: 2026-09-29
published: true
---
while I sit in the garden,
nothing in the world
to write, your shade sits
calmly on the patio, reading 
or dozing, or ear-phones in
watching videos about guitar pedals.
he’s enjoying the cool air
and idleness, he makes the
perfect man of leisure, as 
you would have done.

and so I carry him from
garden to garden, seeing much
more of the evening garden
than I used to, and I write 
to animate the thought
that I’m spending this evening 
as I should have all the others
