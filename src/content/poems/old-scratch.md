---
title: Old Scratch
date: 2023-12-29
published: true
---
There’s an old voice at my ear,
chews daily at my hands,
makes me pick and tear at scabs,
pulling up hunks of flesh.

A scratch a day, keeps the 
nails bloodied and fresh.
It’s honest this way,
wounds kept in plain view.

I still hover over them, but
now I run my nails over 
the surface but don’t dig. 
Whatever’s in there stays put.

I imagine sometimes, an 
unmarked body. Clear 
clean skin, still smooth 
to the touch. 

and then I step out
of the shower
and the moment dissipates 
into the cool air
