I have it within me I think,
to be just whatever would 
please you.

I came close once but you
know me too well for all that,
no thanks to me .

I think when I grow up 
I want to be you so I don't
look to close.

I love you like I've loved 
lots of things, but give
me a second

I can do it better than that
