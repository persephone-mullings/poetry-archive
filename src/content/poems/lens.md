Golden Lab, Bounding-keen,

you leapt, fetch-frenzied, into the stream, just

a few yards beyond my shot.



Your triumphal splashing caught

on footage but nothing 

of your white-gold mane, 

nor your strong bounding paws.



I place you gently, 

in that shadow place with 

all those whose lives I never touched;

skirting the edges of my frame.



My eye, deep-seeking,

fixed downward.
