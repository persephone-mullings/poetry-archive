title: Walking Stick
date: 2023-08-21
published: false

I walk with you
in the spaces between
the clacks of your 
stick against the road.

Your pace has me in 
something like a limp, a 
phantom wound, sewn shut
with thread that ties me

to you. I hover slower 
behind the others with 
you, my silent thievery,
stealing this moment 

that else I’d lose to my
hurry and downcast eyes.
The time I have left with
you could be hours

or seconds. Let me linger 
at the back of the group 
with you, and make jokes
we won’t remember.
