And the young boy,
your uncle,
raises it and nurses it back to health. 

You tell it with a knowing edge,
anticipate my gentle teasing. A story
so full of bucolic, old world charm

it almost sketches itself in rough
pencilled lines inside the book. 
We relate in this way, trading

our country stories, mine perhaps
a little dimmer, mostly second hand. 
In yours I’m drawn over hills

down into old valleys, to somewhere
that shines like memory I lack and 
half remember the smell of.
