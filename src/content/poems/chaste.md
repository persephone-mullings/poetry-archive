I've no art for the chaste kiss. I try, badly, to 
mimic your easy, small brushes, and feel 
all the more lumbering trying. Where did I 
learn this? When did I start to flinch at my 
own arms, reaching. Who decided that my 
body was dangerous, forceful, grasping?
When did I start keeping myself on so short 
a lead? When did I last run my thumb over 
your knuckle, and not bite down the urge to 
apologise? When did I first feel a shiver of 
fear of a dangerous body I didn't control? 
Whatever I make of myself, waiting for me, 
sewn to my core, the wolf in sheep's clothing.
