---
title: Cairn Building
date: 2022-04-21
published: true
---
Laid out, word by word
I build my art,
I round off the
edges like stone,
defying its
form with brittle-smooth
curve.
Layer on layer, idly at first
then driven, clear and
apposite into place.
The cairn takes shape
and under buried I leave
an ache behind.
Amputated.

Not to trouble me again with phantom pains.
