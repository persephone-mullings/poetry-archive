A walk in a park whose 
name I won’t remember.
A furl of streets

unspool beneath me until 
I am there. The bird enclosure
situates me at the heart

of the park and the lapping sound
lights me to the fountain.
The park bending around me

leaves me unphased, but the 
tilting ground pulls at me
my ancestors were a sailing people

probably, so resilient I make 
my way back and collapse 
onto the sofa, and whistle 

as it summersaults beneath me.
If I dreamed, I dreamed of 
swimming pools

I dream of cold guest rooms and 
the welcoming arms of grey 
velvet Kalypso crawling up the walls.
