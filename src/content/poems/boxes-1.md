and so you find yourself trying
to live a life in boxes. You’re
split; part of you is here and
present, the rest is packed away
in attics, spare rooms, tucked 
Under beds, kept out of the way.

and you live like this, because
you must, but you find you’re 
always chasing your shadow, 
“sorry” you’ll say, “I’m miles away.” 
A life on pause, fraying at the edges,
unpacking yourself as needed.

and everything you do, you’re 
working twice as hard, holding the
pieces of your life together enough
to get through. You’re quieter than
before; it’s easier. You’re tireder 
too. Waiting to be you, here, again.
