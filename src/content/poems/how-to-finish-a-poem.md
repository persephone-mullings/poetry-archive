---
title: How to finish a poem
date: 2022-04-21
published: true
---
I was taught to end a poem
opening outward, but that
advice never helps much.

I always tell people
write the poem, then
you end with something

incongruous but poignant.
Just a familiar moment
like

‘and then you step out
of the shower
and the feeling dissipates 
into the cool air’
