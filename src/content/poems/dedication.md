---
title: Dedication
date: 2026-10-04
blurb: For Spike Mullings
published: true
---
He's always in profile. 
I'm following alongside, always
following, and I pester him.
 
I'll ask something tangential, 
feeling my way through for a rich vein- 
music was often that- just to hear him talk. 

He gestures as he does, eyes forward, 
his whole affect is light and easy, 
belying his fierce intelligence. 

So often when I'm walking now, 
I'll follow after,
and share my thoughts with him.
