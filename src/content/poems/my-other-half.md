title: My Other Half
date: 2026-09-21
blurb: Not still dead, not yet entirely ...
published: true
---
Not still dead, not yet entirely 
living. A strange series of masks,
a woman passing as a corpse
passing as a man.

My life is kept somewhere else.
I catch glimpses of it, but like
dust in sunlight, it vanishes at
a turn. I breathe it in with 

one working lung. The other held
in clenched fist, immobile, 
pending official assessment and 
properly ordered permission.

But still I breathe. Half alive and 
dead are much the same to them
but I cherish my gaunt life
and count every half heartbeat.
