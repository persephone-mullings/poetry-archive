title: Phantom Limbs
date: 2026-09-21
blurb: Knitting phantom scarves for...
published: true

Knitting phantom scarves for
far away cousins once removed
or sewing thread through 
spine, a book bound for
father’s day, trading paint,
laid thick and rough on 
board with a friend
heavy hands soft and 
ink stained crave work with
texture: craft. Honest affections
wrought in wood or wool.
Treated leather rough with use 
and lasting a lifetime 
and lying where it’s dropped
pride of place in motions of 
every day.
