The tides I put away, packed
tight and wrapped in plastic are now
coming back out. Out again 
with the boxes and bags 
and I sit at their bottom
again, tossed to and fro
like nothing changes.

Now I live in the spare room of my
life, only half unpacked, waiting ever
for the next move. Possessions stacked
up against the walls like sandbags 
but they stink of brine and 
I have to resist draping sheets over
them to keep the dust of me out.
