---
title: Waiting
date: 2023-09-21
published: true
---
As a child I always  
was scared of crickets. We  
would go often to some park
or another — London’s full of 
them — and, keen as I was,
there was always a reticence.
They lurked in tall grasses, waiting 
out of sight. The sound was 
the worst of it. This constant 
droning, sourceless and too close.
Summer was always a haunted
season, something always there
waiting behind the light. 
I look back now at
that scared child, that fear kept in,
let go of my own.
