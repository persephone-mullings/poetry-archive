The sound is far from your house
but on the walk to your door I 
see an island, thin scrabble of 
land, a light house at one end.

My dreams go back to illegal 
barbeques at the water’s edge. 
Turns taken blowing life into 
glowing coals. 

I promised to stay, and I 
will, but you must forgive 
my farawayness in quiet 
moments. And in return

I’ll come back again.
Again, across the waters,
heave the ocean from 
my lungs, each and 

every time.
