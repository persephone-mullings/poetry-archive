There are mugs at home, 
sat on a shelf, little plates too,
with bird egg and nest designs,
never used. You lament them and
always I say, when I’m more 
settled. When I’m more settled.
I’ll bring them out here and 
they’ll have pride of place.

The park is full of brambles, I 
want to go out and pick them,
like we used to, make jams, wines
crumbles. Theres nothing 
stopping me, its just, there’s 
so much on. One day it’ll 
quieten down a little. Once I’ve
gotten through all this. 

One day I will bring out the 
patterns and thread. Reteach 
myself to sew. Borrow a machine
and allow myself to take time 
to make things. This planning
is new to me, and I wonder 
what time will I do this all in?

And it hits me, I don’t think I
have time. Maybe I don’t.
Maybe just its more of this
from here on out.
