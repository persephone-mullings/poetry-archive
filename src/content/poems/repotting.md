Fingernails faintly green for 
the soil underneath.

I watch, silent, as you dig
through, gently pulling back

layer on layer of earth, seeking
out the roots, as leaves fall

off, an audible clip each time.
He looks healthy but 

that’s no sign often enough.
Could be dead, could be alive.

Run the soil through your fingers,
Scoop rough handfuls into a smaller pot.

I sit with you in silence, 
entirely still for fear I may also 

come apart in your hands.
Will you sort my leaves in two

piles? One dead, one alive.
